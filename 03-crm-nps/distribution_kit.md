## Response-volume targets (rule of thumb)

- **Minimum for a defensible NPS**: ~50 valid responses (after filtering out "No" on Q1).
- **Good for the 3-6 segment comparison in Part 2**: aim for ~15-20 responses per
  magazine title you want to compare, so no single segment is being judged on a
  handful of answers.
- Push hardest in the first 48 hours — response rate drops off fast after that; the
  one-time re-share after ~5-7 days (see WhatsApp tip) is meant to catch the second wave.
